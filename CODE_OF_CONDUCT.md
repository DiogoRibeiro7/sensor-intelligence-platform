# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to keep discussions respectful, technical, and constructive.
Examples of expected behavior include:

- focusing feedback on the work, not the person
- giving concrete reproduction details for bugs
- accepting maintainer decisions once tradeoffs have been explained
- making space for questions and corrections

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block contributors for
behavior that makes the project unsafe or unproductive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`.
